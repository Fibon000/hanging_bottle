The Hanging Bottle Challenge

Dive in, watch the arrows, and discover what a scale really measures.

🧭 Mission directions

⏱ 5 minutes
  1. In air. Press In air. Read the scale (kg) and write it in your manual.
  2. Half in. Press Half in. Watch the arrows, then record the reading.
  3. Fully under. Press Fully under and record the reading.
  4. Dive! Press Dip it! and notice which arrow changes and which stays the same.
  5. Discuss. What could each colored arrow be? Do not press Reveal until your teacher says so.
